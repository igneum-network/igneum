Igneum Miner

Runs a node and mines Igneum with your graphics card. Set up in two steps.

01
Runs in the background
The window can close. The miner keeps going from the menu bar or the tray.
02
Uses your graphics card
Each card is tuned for hashes per watt by itself. You can tune again any time.
03
Pays an address you own
Every block this machine finds pays one address: one made here, or one you paste.

Nobody from Igneum will ever ask for your key.
