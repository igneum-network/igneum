The registry on the research chain

The research chain holds its own registry: which workloads it runs, which of them earn a share of each block, and which lanes its breadth rule balances. Every change is a transaction from the registry key to the registry address, in force from the height its document names. This page reads the chain and nothing else.

The research chain: research tokens with no monetary value. Times are UTC.

Read it yourself

Against any node of the research chain (replace the host; the registry answers at a height or at latest, and each landing's document is the transaction's input after the four-byte selector):

curl -s http://HOST:PORT -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"igneum_vectorRegistry","params":["latest"]}'
curl -s http://HOST:PORT -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_getTransactionByHash","params":["TX_HASH"]}'

The file this page renders is registry.json, rewritten from the chain every thirty seconds by the reader whose source sits beside this page.
