PASS against ADR-0001 threshold (1): the two-in two-out spend proof (money-spend-v1) is 99,980 bytes median over the lab's 25 real transfers (max 101,676; the bench's 99,916) at **128 conjectured bits**, the verifier's own reading, 29% under the 140 KB bound; the bound is the conjectured one (p3-security's proximity-gaps budget, ePrint 2025/2010's random-words rate for the query round), named as such, not a proven one.

## 1. The route taken, in the order of preference the brief set

1. **A proof option the pinned library already supports.** None. Miden VM 0.36.1 exposes one proof configuration (miden-air 0.36.1 src/config.rs: LOG_BLOWUP 3, NUM_QUERIES 27, QUERY_POW_BITS 17, DEEP_POW_BITS 12, FOLDING_POW_BITS 4, the quadratic extension of Goldilocks as the challenge field, fixed in the type `MidenStarkConfig = GenericStarkConfig<Felt, QuadFelt, ...>`), no preset, no builder, no feature; the CLI refuses any other. The join's section 5 showed that no query count or grinding moves the lookup-challenge term past 108 bits for this statement, because that term is `log2|E| - log2((W + 2) * fractions_per_row) - log_max_height` with |E| the 128-bit quadratic field: 127.99997 - 8.98 - 11 = 108.02. A larger query count or a lower blowup only moves the query term. So the first route is closed by the library's shape, not by a missing constant.
2. **The smallest patch to the pinned release**, taken: the cubic extension F_p[X]/(X^3 - X - 1) as the Miden VM proof's challenge field (192 bits), 38 FRI queries, the lookup-boundary correction applied before the transcript cap. Five crates of the crates.io release 0.36.1 (tag v0.36.1, commit 70bf609ade95a21d811045ee8d66fffe5d5a48dc) are copied from the cargo registry at build time and patched by `tools/money/stage1-prover/prepare-patched.sh` from `tools/money/stage1-prover/patches/<crate>.patch`; the workspace's `[patch.crates-io]` builds the prover, the verifier and the node's verifier library from the copies; nothing patched is committed but the diffs. No statement, witness, hash, encoding or vector changed: the shared vectors (tools/money/vectors/money-stage1-vectors.json) and the program hashes are the join's, and the prover's two tests pass unchanged. The soundness assumptions of the statements are untouched; what changed is the field the STARK's random challenges are drawn from and the number of FRI queries.

## 2. The formula and its inputs (the verifier's own reading)

The verifier grades every proof it verifies with p3-security 0.8.0's conjectured round budget (`p3_security::budget::security_report`, miden-air security.rs `ProofSecurityParameters::conjectured_security_report`), the minimum over seven terms, each `log2|E| - log2(coefficient) - log2(size) + grinding bits`, capped at `min(log2|E|, the commitment hash's collision resistance)`, in Q16 fixed point, whole bits rounded down. "Conjectured" is the library's word: list sizes taken as one up to capacity, the query round at the random-words rate of ePrint 2025/2010 section 1.5; proven bounds for this parameter class are far lower and not modelled.

The inputs for the spend proof (the shield's are the same but the trace height, which is also 2^11): log_max_height 11; the quadratic field 127.99997 bits (Q16 8,388,606), the cubic 191.99995 (Q16 12,582,909); the commitment hash BLAKE3-256, collision resistance 128; the lookup shape (W + 2) x fractions_per_row = (16 + 2) x 28 = 504 (8.98 bits) plus the boundary correction log2(1 + 3 / (28 x 2^11)) = 0.00005 bits; LOG_BLOWUP 3; grinding 17 (query), 12 (DEEP), 4 (folding), 0 (lookup, out-of-domain).

| term | quadratic field, 31 queries (the join) | cubic field, 38 queries (this cut) | the arithmetic at the cubic field |
|---|---|---|---|
| lookup-challenge | 108 | 128 (cap; 172.0 uncapped) | 191.99995 - 8.98 - 11 - 0.00005 |
| constraint-composition | 119 | 128 (cap; 183.3) | 191.99995 - log2(428) |
| out-of-domain | 113 | 128 (cap; 177.5) | 191.99995 - log2(9 + 2) - 11 |
| deep-composition | 119 | 128 (cap; 182.7) | 191.99995 - log2(num_deep_terms) - 14 + 12 |
| fri-folding | 115 | 128 (cap; 179.4) | 191.99995 - log2(6) - 14 + 4 |
| fri-query | 108 | 128 (cap; 129.7) | 38 x 2.967 + 17 |
| commitment-collision | 127 | 128 | min(191.99995, 128) |
| **conjectured level** | **108** | **128** | |

The bits a query is worth: `bits_per_query(log_blowup, |E|) = log_blowup - ceil_log2((|E| + log2 e + log_blowup) / |E|)`, 2.951 over the quadratic field, 2.967 over the cubic (the correction shrinks as the field grows). With QUERY_POW_BITS 17: 37 queries read 126.8, 38 read 129.7; 38 is the least count that clears the cap at the release's grinding. The alternatives not taken: 37 queries with 19 bits of grinding, or 36 with 22, read 128 too and save 2.4 to 4.8 KB a proof against 4x to 32x the grinding work for every prover; a blowup of 16 would halve the query count at twice the proving time and memory. One knob moved is the honest minimum; the trade is the panel's.

**The one change to the estimator itself, and why.** The release computes the lookup term, caps it, then subtracts the boundary correction (`apply_lookup_correction`), so a lookup round the cap binds reads 128 - 0.00005 = 127.99992 bits, 127 whole bits, whatever the field: under the release's reporter no Miden VM proof can read 128 whole bits under a 128-bit commitment hash (the first build of this cut read exactly that: lookup-challenge 127, Q16 8,388,603, every other term 128). The correction belongs to the round's error (`M = fractions_per_row x 2^H + boundary terms`, the comment on `lookup_boundary_correction` says so), so the patch subtracts it from the round's uncapped bound and applies the cap last, as the budget applies the cap to every other round. Below the cap the two orders agree to the bit (the join's 108 reproduces); at the cap the difference is 2^-13 bits. This is a change to the reporter's order of operations, not to any bound; it is the first item of section 6.

## 6. What the panel must re-check, in order

1. The reporter's order of operations (section 2's last paragraph): that subtracting the lookup-boundary correction before the cap is the budget's meaning, and that reading 128 rather than 127.99992 whole bits is the right reading of the same bound. If the review holds the release's order to be the one of record, the figure of record is 127 bits and the verdict reads "128 - 2^-13", which is the cap less a rounding, not a weaker round.
2. The cubic field's instance: X^3 - X - 1 irreducible over Goldilocks (p3-goldilocks's sage comment), the Frobenius matrix and the generator 2 + X delegated from p3-goldilocks 0.8.0, the two-adicity 32; the lifted STARK's FRI folding, DEEP batching, out-of-domain evaluation and challenger sampling (three base elements a challenge) generic over the field. No test of the pinned release runs a Miden VM proof over this field; the evidence is this cut's 101 verified records, the seven refusal rows and the prover's tests.
3. The three cubic constants that enter the report by the patch: MVM_CHALLENGE_FIELD_BITS (Q16 12,582,909), MVM_EXTENSION_DEGREE 3 in the DEEP term count, EXT_DIMENSION 3 in the memory model.
4. The precompile VM and the recursive verifier stay on the quadratic field: a Miden proof carrying precompile proofs, or the in-VM recursive verification of a Miden VM proof, is outside this patch (the recursive path would deserialise a cubic proof as quadratic and fail). Money uses neither.
5. The 38-query choice against the alternatives of section 2.
6. The one line outside tools/money/stage1-prover/ and this page: `7409: 1` in params.IgneumChainPoolFrom (the lab table), which the gated lab needs and the brief's recipe requires; the live chains' tables untouched. And the join page's threshold-1 row, changed by reference only.
7. The lab's count: 25 transfers, not the join's 101; the sizes and times above agree with the bench to the percent.
