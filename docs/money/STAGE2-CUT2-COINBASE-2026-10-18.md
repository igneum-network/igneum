# Money stage two, cut two: the shielded coinbase (a sealer's reward minted into the pool, the amount public, the owner hidden)

**PASS** as a proof of concept on the lab chain 7405 (lab box A, 18 October 2026, 22:17 to 22:20 UTC): two sealers, one registered, 121 blocks, 53 coinbase notes minted to the wei against the issuance, two of them spent (one private transfer, one unshield) through the real verifier, seven ledger identities reconciled with none failing, both nodes on one root. The branch lead/money-coinbase from main c6037f6a2; every live chain's rules byte-identical (the gate params.IgneumChainPoolFrom is zero on every chain of record; the lab chain 7405 alone is at 1; TestCoinbaseGateOffEveryChainOfRecord). Nothing here changes "Money remains research" (docs/money/PANEL-READING-2-2026-10-18.md): the stage-one conditions stand. No em dash appears on this page. No price or earning figure appears or can be derived.

The spec of record: docs/money/PAYOUT-PRIVACY-SPEC-2026-10-18.md, the chosen option (b): a public amount, a hidden owner, no ciphertext, no proof. Four places where the built form departs from the spec's words are listed in section 3; each keeps the spec's property (the node verifies the mint by arithmetic from public data alone) and none weakens what is hidden.

## 1. What this cut is

- **The registration** (node/consensus/igneum/pool/coinbase.go): a transaction of no value to the pool address whose data is the tag 0x03 then the wallet's 1,227-byte address (pk, ek, d; `igneum-money address --raw`): 1,228 bytes. Finalize stores it under the sender's account (the pool's storage: pk one slot, ek 37 slots, d one slot). Refused with the value returned: a registration carrying value, a zero pk, a pk that is not a word, an ek that is not 1,184 bytes or fails FIPS 203's encapsulation-key check (crypto/mlkem, Go 1.27). The tag alone clears the registration. A registration or a clearing counts from the next block (section 3, item 3).
- **The mint** (pool_settle.go, consensus.go): under the landing check, when the block's coinbase account holds a registered key as the block begins, the sealer share of the plain form (no payout list: the whole miner part, the coordinate's accrued share, or the quarter under PayV2, whichever the chain pays) is withheld from the public credit by `paySealer` and minted by `settlePool` after the block's shields, spends and registrations: r = B("Igneum Money coinbase mint", chain id, height, parent hash, sealer) reduced to a word (BLAKE3 derive_key, the wallet's B), cm = H(H(v, pk), r) (the ordinary note form, Poseidon2 over Goldilocks), the commitment appended to the tree, the pool address credited v, the turnstile and a new `minted` sum raised by v, and the record (sealer, value by leaf index; leaf index by height) written under the pool address. The fund's one percent is credited publicly, unchanged. A sealer without a registration is credited publicly as before. On both assembly and import Finalize runs the same path, so the two sealers and the second node computed one root (identity M6).
- **The ledger's figures**: `igneum_pool` gains `minted`; `igneum_supply` gains `mintedShielded` (the part of `issued` that was minted; absent where the pool is off); `igneum_poolBlock` gains `mints` (position, sealer, value, randomness, commitment); `igneum_poolPayoutAddress(account)` serves a registration (an outside pool reads it as the node does). The turnstile's identity is now turnstile = shielded in + minted in minus unshielded out minus fees burned = the pool address's balance.
- **The wallet** (wallet/money): `scan` reads a block's `mints`, rebuilds the note (v, pk, r, d zero, no memo) when the pk is its own and cm matches, and holds it as an ordinary note with the ordinary nullifier H(H(nk, cm), position); no ciphertext is read, no decapsulation runs. `address --raw` prints the registration's bytes. A minted note is spent by `spend-prepare` and money-spend-v1 exactly as a shielded one.
- **The lab driver** node/cmd/money-coinbase-lab and the stage script tools/money/lab/pool-stage-coinbase.sh; the record docs/money/records/coinbase-7405/ (identity.txt in the settlement ledger's identity form, result.json).

## 3. Where the built form departs from the spec's words

1. **The record lives in the pool's state, not in a block-body field.** The spec's section 4.2 names a mint record "beside withdrawals". Every field of the record (sealer, value, r, cm, position) is a function of the header, the chain id and the registry, so a body field would carry nothing a node cannot recompute; the state root covers the record and the block format is unchanged (no RLP change, no header change, nothing for a reader of the live chains to learn). igneum_poolBlock serves it. The spec's byte figure (60 a key of body) becomes a state figure (section 4).
2. **r's inputs are (chain id, height, parent hash, sealer), not (chain id, block hash, window index, key).** The block hash is not known when Finalize runs on assembly (the seal comes after); the parent hash and the height fix the block as well, and the wallet reads both from the header.
3. **A registration counts from the next block, not the next window.** The plain form has no windows; the key is read before the block's records on assembly and on import alike, so a registration or a clearing carried by block N first applies at N+1 on every node (TestCoinbaseMintUnderGate).
4. **The per-window payout list is not built in this cut** (section 6).

## 4. The figures

- **Bytes per coinbase note**: the block body +0. The state: four slots written per note (the leaf, the sealer by index, the value by index, the index by height: 4 x 32 bytes of values under hashed keys, about 256 bytes before the trie's overhead) plus the frontier's amortised two; the registration 39 slots once per sealer. ## 5. What a watcher sees (the linkability check)

The watcher holds every block and every record. It sees:

- **The registration at 18**: a transaction from the sealer's account to the pool address carrying the 1,227-byte address. It learns: this sealer pays its reward to this pk (and this ek). This is public by design (the spec's section 8: "so is the key's registered shielded address").
- **Every block the sealer seals after 18**: the coinbase, and the mint record (sealer, value, position, r, cm). It learns: the sealer earned exactly this value at this block, and leaf cm at this position is the sealer's. This is the spec's "the amount a key earned per window is public, as today".
- **The transfer at 120**: a transaction from the carrier (the faucet account, not the sealer) to the pool address: an anchor, two nullifiers, two output commitments with ciphertexts, public value 0, a fee. Nothing in it is the sealer's address, a minted commitment, a leaf position or a value. The nullifier of a minted note is H(H(nk, cm), position) under the sealer's nullifier key nk, which the registration does not carry (the address is pk, ek, d, and nk is not derivable from pk = H(sk_spend, nk)), so the watcher holding every mint record cannot compute any minted note's nullifier: **nothing on the ledger ties the spend to a block or to the sealer.** The carrier's identity is the stage-one caveat (F-761's signerless type, open).
- **The unshield at 121**: the same form with a public value and a public output. It learns: some note worth at least that value left the pool to that address, not which note and not whose. **The amount correlation of the spec's section 8 stands**: the unshield's public value equals one note's value less the fee, and every minted value is on the ledger, so a watcher comparing them finds the match (here, every S1 block after the registration minted the same 5,900,859,832,763,671,875, so the match is "one of 53 sealer notes, or anyone else's note of that size", not a block). A miner who unshields its reward amounts verbatim links them; a private transfer gives nothing. The measurement the spec names (K-05 and K-06, before and after, by amount and timing) is not this cut's.
- **What stays public**: that the sealer mines, every block it sealed, every amount it earned, and the pk it pays to. **What is hidden**: everything after the mint: the balance, the spend time, the payee, whether and when it was unshielded, beyond what an amount repeated verbatim gives away.

## 6. The per-window payout rail (the design note for item 3 of the brief)

Not built in this cut. Under a payout list (the share lane, mineshares.go; the Pay rule's three settle paths: settleMinedShares, SettleSharesV2 and payProRata in sortition.go) each listed key is credited its pro rata part at the list's block; the mint form for a listed key is the spec's section 4.2 exactly (per key: r_K, cm_K, the pool credit in place of the 0x credit, the record per key; the fund public), and its cost rule is the spec's section 7 (the batched insertion of n consecutive leaves at about n + 64 merges, against 32 n by the one-leaf form; the G1 budget on the minimum node at n = 1,000). It belongs to the the node team's settle paths, not to the coinbase path this cut was scoped to, and it needs the batched `append` first (the one-leaf form at 1,000 keys is 32,000 merges a list block). The outside pool's payout (section 4.3, S_payout, up to 64 outputs in one shielding transaction from the pool's public account, the members' amounts hidden, the pool's total public) is a third statement on the prover's machinery (money-spend-v1 is 2-in 2-out) and the pool team's cut, not the node's: the registry it reads is this cut's igneum_poolPayoutAddress. Both are named here so no claim rests on them.

## 7. Caveats

- A fault found and cured on the path, **F-797** by the review's third reading (docs/money/PANEL-READING-3-2026-10-19.md): **the pool account with no balance was cleared as empty at the block's end** (EIP-161: nonce 0, balance 0, no code), its storage with it. The first lab run registered at block 15 and the registration was gone by 16 (no mint, igneum_poolPayoutAddress null). The join never met it because a shield's value lands before any pool write; a pool whose every note is unshielded to a zero balance would have lost its tree and its nullifier set the same way (then a fresh shield restarts the tree at count 0 under an anchor history that is gone). The cure: `touch` sets the pool account's nonce to 1 on its first write (every admission that writes: shield, spend, registration, mint), under the landing check only; TestRegistrationRules checks the root survives IntermediateRoot with deletion on. The live chains at gate zero never reach it.
- Two CPU sealers at one difficulty fork often (three reorganisations on each node in 126 blocks): the driver reads a registration's receipt again after both nodes hold it. A mint in a dropped block is dropped with its state, as any credit is.
- The plain form's sealer share alone is minted: the coordinate and PayV2 variants go through the same `paySealer` but were not exercised on 7405 (the dev chain pays the v1 plain form; identity M1 reads igneum_blockIssuance's credited whichever the form).
- The registration is from the coinbase account's own key (an EVM transaction, gas from that account). A sealer whose etherbase is an address it cannot sign from cannot register; Ember's registration flow (the spec's "offers, never sends") is not this cut's.
- The amount is public and repeated verbatim per block, so the unshield correlation above; d is still F-756's zero diversifier, so a sealer that registers the pk it also receives payments to has linked the two (the spec's section 8).
- The N100 figure is an ESTIMATE; the 108-bit setting of the release stands; the per-window list, S_payout and the batched insertion are not built; the link measurement K-05/K-06 is not run.
- This cut added no block-body field; if the panel wants the record in the body for header-only readers, it is one RLP field and a re-cut of igneum_poolBlock, not a change of rule.
