# the review's reading of 18 October 2026: ADR-0001 and the two chip tiles

One session, the five roles in turn (the review lead chair, the security review, the security review and B, the design review), read at about 18:0x UK on 18 October 2026. The faults are numbered from F-756 (the register's last number at the freeze was F-755). No em dash appears on this page. Sources opened for this reading are named where they were opened.

## 1. ADR-0001 (docs/money/ADR-0001-PRIVATE-PQ-SAFE-MONEY-2026-10-18.md, read at ea1b1a088, rebased onto main 33bd0c779)

**Ruling: PASS WITH RE-CUTS, eight wording faults, applied by the review in one commit on lead/money-adr. The ADR's own verdict, "Money remains research", stands and binds.**

The questions put to the review, answered:

- The hardest question (privacy, PQ ownership and node cost in one construction) is answered honestly and with sources: section 4.3 names the exact statement, keys, encryption and turnstile; section 4.4 says what stays protected and what does not, and names ML-KEM as the one assumption outside the hash family; the Orchard, Monero and Quantus comparisons are cited to pages that say what is quoted (the panel opened zcash/zcash issue 4946 for the 9,160 bytes, ePrint 2026/861 for the 79.8 KiB figure and the disproof of the up-to-capacity conjecture, arXiv 2603.07974 for the 107 KB and 1.1 ms figures, pkg.go.dev crypto/mlkem for the ML-KEM-768 sizes, and Candide's recovery page for the guardians, the threshold and the owner's cancellation; each page says what the ADR quotes).
- Sizes and costs are sourced or labelled: every unmeasured figure carries ESTIMATE or APPROXIMATE, and the arithmetic of section 4.5 (the address 1,227 bytes, the ciphertext 1,300, the state 2.75 KB a transfer, 2.6 MB of ciphertexts a day at 1,000 transfers, the 128 GB SSD filled in three to five months at 10,000 a day with proofs kept) re-computes. One figure was wrong (F-762).
- The stop condition binds: section 4.6 states five numbered thresholds, each a measured row on the research chain, and until they pass "no privacy claim, no PQ claim, no vault built, and no served page states the design as running". Verdict in one line: the stop condition binds, and the ADR applies it to itself.
- The vault's authority separation is real as a specification (section 2.2 and the theft-containment row of section 3: the everyday key can never shorten a delay, raise a limit or replace the recovery authority; the cancellation key can cancel and cannot redirect, spend or change policy) and is undemonstrated, as the ADR says; one sentence left the authority for policy changes unnamed (F-757). The vault's construction inside a private pool (how a pending withdrawal is visible to the cancellation key's holder without being visible to the ledger) is stage 5's paper and is not claimed here.
- The mining-payout leak is specified before any miner-privacy claim: section 5 names the direct shielded coinbase, with the note randomness derived from public data and the amount public, and withholds the claim until the minted value reconciles to the wei and the link measurement runs.
- The comparison table hides nothing behind an aggregate: no score, every cell with its basis, and section 9.1 names the one cell that differs.
- The four measured wins are the bar for every claim: section 10 withholds the superiority sentence until the four are measured and published, names the comparable systems, and keeps the GPU system as a separate advantage.
- Process, price and value words: none, after F-760; no earning, price or value figure appears or can be derived.
- Claims ahead of measurement: two absolute sentences (F-758), one false-as-constructed claim (F-756), one stale figure (F-759); each re-cut.
- "Why a chain and not a wallet": sound. A policy that must bind a thief who holds the everyday key cannot live in a wallet the thief does not use; it must be enforced by consensus, inside the pool's spend statement, and no existing pool's circuit carries one. The panel adds that the argument proves the vault needs a chain; it does not by itself prove the vault needs this chain rather than a new circuit on Zcash or Firo, and the ADR says as much in section 1.0 item 4 (those chains could add one only by a protocol change). That is the honest form.

### The faults and the re-cuts (applied)

- **F-756 (red team, the security review): a privacy claim false as constructed.** Section 4.4's table row "Address unlinkability from the address itself" read "PROTECTED; diversified addresses (different d) under one seed are unlinkable unless the ciphertexts fall as above." As section 4.3 item 1 defines the address, (pk, ek, d) carries pk = H(sk_spend, nk) and the ML-KEM key ek in the clear for every d, so two of a holder's addresses are linkable by inspection; the diversifier does nothing. Re-cut: the row now reads OPEN, states the linkability as written, names the per-d derivation (pk_d from pk and d inside the circuit, a KEM key pair per d, the wallet scanning against every d it has issued) as the stage 1 design item, and claims no unlinkability between one holder's addresses until it is settled and measured.
- **F-757 (chair, the security review): the authority for a policy change unnamed.** Section 2.2 read "Policy changes (delays, limits, the recovery authority) need stronger authority than the everyday key and take their own delay before they apply." Sections 7 and 9 assume the recovery authority. Re-cut: "need the recovery authority, never the everyday key alone (the form sections 7 and 9 already assume), and take their own delay before they apply; what a vault with no recovery authority set may change, and under which key, is a stage 5 design item and is not decided here."
- **F-758 (chair, the design review): two absolute sentences ahead of review.** Section 1.1 read "so a QC can never forge a spend or a recovery." Re-cut: "so no QC forges a spend or a recovery by solving a discrete logarithm; what remains is the hash and lattice assumptions listed in section 4.3, and nothing is claimed beyond them." Section 4.4 read "The sentence this table licenses, once reviewed: a QC adversary can never take funds from the pool or forge a recovery;". Re-cut: "The sentence this table licenses, once reviewed and under the assumptions of section 4.3: a QC adversary cannot take funds from the pool or forge a recovery;".
- **F-759 (chair): a stale figure against the audit of record.** Section 4.2 (d) and the "Can a chip take the mining" row of section 9 carried "the 20x to 300x figure APPROXIMATE" from the morning's reading, while main 5743ce601 carries the Quantus chip audit (a chip at about 47x by the model, the band 23x to 62x, modelled, none built). Both places and the sources list now cite docs/resistance/QUANTUS-CHIP-AUDIT-2026-10-18.md with its figures; "double Poseidon2 squeeze" is now "one Poseidon2 sponge over Goldilocks with a double squeeze", the chain's own code comment (qpow-math/src/lib.rs: a single Poseidon2 hash with double squeeze).
- **F-760 (the design review): process words.** Section 4.5 read "(Polygon Miden figures as reported in the search summary, APPROXIMATE)". Re-cut: "(Polygon Miden figures as reported on the forum page in section 11, APPROXIMATE)".
- **F-761 (red team, the security review): the shielded transaction's fee path unstated.** Section 4.1 says every transaction is signed with secp256k1 ECDSA; a fully shielded transfer has no public signer and section 4.3 did not say how its fee is paid or how the turnstile counts it. Re-cut, added to section 4.3 item 4: "A fully shielded transaction therefore carries no public signer: it is a new transaction type the node accepts with no ECDSA signature, whose fee is the statement's public fee paid from the pool's value (the base fee burned, the tip to the sealer, under the fee rule of section 4.1), and whose relay and mempool rules are a stage 1 design item." And item 7's turnstile now counts "value shielded in (and, after section 5, minted in) minus value unshielded out and fees paid out of the pool".
- **F-762 (chair): one figure wrong.** Section 4.6 read "a year of 10,000-a-day history is about 16 hours of download". At 85 to 145 KB a transfer, 3,650,000 transfers are 310 to 530 GB, which at 50 Mbit/s is 14 to 24 hours; 16 hours was the bottom of the range read as the whole. Re-cut: "310 to 530 GB, 14 to 24 hours of download".
- **F-763 (chair): a stale branch reference.** Section 4.1 and the sources list cited docs/tokenomics/SUPPLY-2026-10-18.md "on lead/supply-1b-docs"; the file landed on main at 2b0373054, before the ADR's own reference commit 305dd0431. Re-cut: "on main" in section 4.1, the branch words dropped from the sources list.

The status line now reads "PANEL-READ 18 October 2026 ... PASS with eight wording re-cuts, F-756 to F-763, applied; the verdict of section 4.6 stands: Money remains research." Nothing else on the page changed. Open items the review names for stage 1, not faults in the record: the per-d address derivation (F-756), the shielded transaction type's relay and mempool rules (F-761), the vault's pending state inside a private pool (stage 5), and the in-circuit hash's round count against the 2025 to 2026 cryptanalysis (the ADR's own section 4.7).

## 2. The Quantus tile (docs/resistance/QUANTUS-CHIP-AUDIT-2026-10-18.md on main 5743ce601)

**Ruling: PASS WITH RE-CUTS (two), then served. Grade: modelled, none built, the Ravencoin row's grade.**

Checked: the definitions are the served five's (the chip's hashes a joule over the 4090's on the chain's own hash; the keep is one over the edge). The inputs were opened and say what is quoted: the Kryptex 4090 page (Quantus 1.35 GH/s at 380 W), the 5090 page (1.81 GH/s at 525 W), the CMP 170HX page (365.38 MH/s at 175 W); qpow-math/src/lib.rs (a 32-byte block hash and a 64-byte nonce, 96 bytes, one Poseidon2 hash with a double squeeze to 512 bits, valid when strictly under U512::MAX divided by the difficulty); qp-poseidon's poseidon2.rs (Goldilocks, width 12, rate 8, capacity 4, 8 external and 22 internal rounds, the S-box x^7). The arithmetic re-computes: 25 elements at rate 8 make four absorbing permutations plus one for the second squeeze, the first precomputable, four a nonce; 118 S-boxes at four multiplies make 472, plus 264 constant multiplies; at 2 pJ and 1 pJ with a quarter added, 1.51 nJ a permutation and 6.0 nJ a nonce, 166 MH/J against the card's 3.55 MH/J: 47x; the low and high cases 4.5 and 12.1 nJ give 62x and 23x; the keep 2.1 percent, the band 1.6 to 4.3.

- **F-764 (chair, the design review): a rival's modelled edge rounded in its own favour and its band narrowed inward.** The audit's own model reads 47x central with the band 23x to 62x and serves "50x (the band 25x to 60x)", while its keep band 1.6 to 4.3 percent is the unrounded band. A modelled figure on another chain's tile is served at the model's own value, as Ravencoin's 3.4x is; Kaspa's 702x to 700x rounds against ourselves and is the only rounding the record allows. Re-cut required on the audit page's tile block and on the served tile: the big figure 47x, the band 23x to 62x.
- **F-765 (the design review): the subtitle names the work by the pool guides' words, not the chain's.** "QPoW, double Poseidon2, none built": the chain's code calls it a single Poseidon2 hash with a double squeeze, and the audit says so in its own Work section. Re-cut: "QPoW, Poseidon2, none built".

**The tile text of record, after the re-cuts:**

- Name: Quantus
- Subtitle: QPoW, Poseidon2, none built
- The big figure, in the page's form: 47x
- What your card keeps: 2%
- Grade words: modelled, none built
- The tile paragraph, in /resistance's form: "47x: a chip earns this many times your card for the same electricity, by the model (the band 23x to 62x). What your card keeps, next to the modelled chip: 2% (modelled, none built)."
- The stat line, in /resistance's form: "47x | Quantus, QPoW, Poseidon2, none built"

The wording is free of accusation: no "fails", no "AI coin", no word about Quantus as money; the tile names a hash and a model, as the Ravencoin tile does.

## 3. The Pearl tile (docs/resistance/PEARL-CHIP-AUDIT-2026-10-18.md on main 5743ce601)

**Ruling: PASS WITH RE-CUTS (three), then served. Grade: spec sheet, with the miners' rows' 1.2x today on the tile itself.**

Checked: the inputs were opened and say what is quoted: the Kryptex 4090 page (Pearl 293 TH/s at 450 W), the 5090 page (400 TH/s at 525 W), the CMP 170HX page (160 TH/s at 200 W); NVIDIA's H100 page (FP8 Tensor Core 3,958 teraFLOPS SXM and 3,341 NVL, the footnote "With sparsity", TDP up to 700 W and 350 to 400 W); the HGX page (HGX B200 FP8 72 PFLOPS, "Dense is half sparse spec shown"); the DGX B200 page (FP8 72 PFLOPS sparse, about 14.3 kW maximum, eight GPUs). The arithmetic re-computes: 1,979 over 700 is 2.83 TFLOPS a watt against the 4090's 330.3 over 450, 0.734: 3.85x; the NVL 4.18 over 0.734: 5.7x; the B200 at 36,000 over 8 over 1,000: 4.50, 6.1x; the DGX box 36,000 over 14,300: 2.52, 3.4x; the keeps 26, 18 and 16 percent. The definitions differ from the served five in one respect the tile must carry (F-766).

- **F-766 (chair, red team): the grade "spec sheet" alone overstates what the figure is, and the 1.2x caveat belongs on the tile.** The served five's spec-sheet figures are a shipped chip's rate on that chain's own hash (the S21 on SHA-256d, the KS5 Pro on kHeavyHash); Pearl's 3.9x to 6.1x is a ratio of the makers' peak dense FP8 figures at the chain's precision, a ceiling the shipped kernels do not reach: the only measured rows on the data-centre parts (SRBMiner's B300 row, 0.767 TH/W against the 4090's 0.651) read 1.2x. A tile that names a live chain's work carries both on the tile, not the audit page alone: the big figure by the spec sheets, the 1.2x of the miners' rows beside it, in the grade words. Re-cut required: the grade words "spec sheet; the miners' rows 1.2x today" on the tile paragraph.
- **F-767 (chair): the HGX parenthetical misreads the page.** The audit's B200 row reads "the 8-GPU HGX B200 figure (36 PFLOPS FP16 dense, 72 FP8)"; the page lists 72 PFLOPS FP8 and 36 PFLOPS FP16/BF16, both sparse, dense half. The figure used, 4,500 dense FP8 a GPU, is right (72 halved, over eight); the words are wrong. Re-cut required on the audit page: "(72 PFLOPS FP8 and 36 FP16 with sparsity, dense half: 36 PFLOPS FP8 over eight GPUs)".
- **F-768 (the design review, the security review): the subtitle's word "NoisyGEMM" is on no page of record.** The audit's Work section quotes the whitepaper as FP8 matrix multiplication with low-rank noise and never the word; the panel opened the whitepaper (pearlresearch.ai/Pearl_Whitepaper.pdf, read 18 October 2026) and found "E4M3FN matrix multiplication" and no "NoisyGEMM". A served subtitle carries a name the record can point to. Re-cut: "pearlhash, FP8 GEMM, the H100 class" (six words; pearlhash is the pool's name for the work, FP8 GEMM the whitepaper's arithmetic, the H100 class the chain's intended miners by its official pool).

**The tile text of record, after the re-cuts:**

- Name: Pearl
- Subtitle: pearlhash, FP8 GEMM, the H100 class
- The big figure, in the page's form: 3.9x to 6.1x (the range form the relation page already serves for 1.2x to 1.4x; the H100 3.9x, the B200 6.1x; never 6.1x alone)
- What your card keeps: 16 to 26%
- Grade words: spec sheet; the miners' rows 1.2x today
- The tile paragraph, in /resistance's form: "3.9x to 6.1x: a chip earns this many times your card for the same electricity, by the makers' FP8 spec sheets (the H100 3.9x, the B200 6.1x). What your card keeps, next to the strongest chip: 16 to 26% (spec sheet; on the miners' own rows the chips read 1.2x today)."
- The stat line, in /resistance's form: "3.9x to 6.1x | Pearl, pearlhash, FP8 GEMM, the H100 class"

The wording is free of accusation: the tile says what the chips are (the makers' AI parts, shipping) and what they read, and nothing about the chain's purpose or its coin.
