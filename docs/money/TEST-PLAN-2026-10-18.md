# Test plan: the adversarial campaign as executable rows, and the four measured wins' harness (Project Money, 18 October 2026)

Status: DESIGN, on paper; PANEL-READ 18 October 2026 (docs/money/PANEL-READING-2-2026-10-18.md: PASS with re-cuts F-777, F-778 and F-786, applied). ADR-0001 section 7 (the campaign) and section 10 (the four measured wins), written as rows an engineer runs without a question. Every row names the attack, the setup, the exact action, the expected refusal or bound, and the evidence file. Nothing here has run. Tests do not establish an unproven construction's security: the campaign finds bugs; the cryptographic review, the published bounds and the measurements of ADR section 4.6 are what any claim rests on (ADR section 7's closing sentence, repeated here as the rule of this page). No em dash appears on this page. No price or earning figure appears or can be derived.

## 1. The decision

One campaign, run once against the prototype on the research chain (7358) or a throwaway chain from the same genesis form, every row with its command and its refusal or its finding, grouped as ADR section 7 lists them (groups A to L below, plus group P for the payout page), and the harness for the four measured wins (section 4) run against the same scenarios on Monero's and Zcash's reference wallets and a Safe-style vault, each with the figures named. A row passes when the expected column is met exactly; any other outcome is a FINDING with a fault number from the review lead's register, never a gap.

## 2. The form of every row and every evidence file

- Setup: the chain, the node build (sha), the wallet build, the keys (test keys only, generated for the row, printed in the evidence file), the heights.
- Action: one command line per step (the wallet's command form, or a raw transaction built by the test tool), reproducible by anyone with the repository.
- Expected: the node's refusal string (named per row; the node's error names are the engineer's to fix and the row's to record), or the bound in the row's own terms.
- Evidence: docs/money/records/campaign-<date>/<row id>/ with record.json (the row id, the setup, the commands, the node's replies, the block heights, PASS or FINDING), the node log excerpt, and the proof bytes where the row makes one.
- Conventions: W the everyday balance, L the period limit, P the period, D the withdrawal delay, R the recovery delay, X the closure delay, as docs/money/VAULT-SPEC-2026-10-18.md section 3.1; S_* the statements of that page; the ADR's S_transfer for ordinary transfers; "refused in the pool" means the node's mempool rejects the transaction; "refused in a block" means a block carrying it is invalid on import by a second node.

The defaults for every vault row unless the row says otherwise: D_b 300, D_s 3,600, P 300, L one tenth of the vault's balance, R_b 600, R_s 7,200, X_b 2,100, X_s 25,200 (the floors, so a row runs in hours, not days; the defaults of the wallet are the spec's).

## 4. The four measured wins' harness

The same scenarios, run against Money (the prototype on 7358), Monero (monero-wallet-cli and monerod of the current release, on stagenet or a private testnet), Zcash (the reference wallet of the day, zcashd's wallet or its successor Zallet, with zebrad, on a private testnet), and a Safe-style vault (Safe{Wallet}'s contracts with the Candide recovery module on a local Ethereum development chain), each with the figures below. No aggregate score; every figure beside the same figure for each system, with the version and the chain named. A figure a system cannot produce (no such key, no such role) is written "no such role" as ADR section 9's table does.

### 4.1 Security (win 1): the compromise scenarios

| scenario | the action on each system | figures to collect per system |
|---|---|---|
| S-1 the everyday key stolen | the thief holds the hot wallet's seed (Money: the everyday seed; Monero and Zcash: the spending key; Safe: one owner key) | the maximum loss as a fraction of the holding (Money: W over (W + vault), per VAULT-SPEC section 9); the time until the owner's first possible counter-action; the number of further secrets the thief would need for the rest |
| S-2 the cancellation key stolen | Money only has one; the others record "no such role" | the loss (expected 0); the denial the thief can cause (E-01's N) |
| S-3 the recovery authority stolen | Money: the guardian keys; Safe: the guardian quorum; others "no such role" | the loss within the delay (expected 0 with a cancellation); the loss after it with no cancellation (Money: everything after R; Safe: everything after the module's delay) |
| S-4 restore from an old backup | the seed restored after spends on every system | the restored balance against the chain's (expected equal everywhere; a stale balance is a FINDING) |
| S-5 the public chain's view during a recovery | Safe: the recovery's guardians, delay and amounts on the ledger; Money: one hash | what the ledger shows, listed |

| scenario | figures to collect per system |
|---|---|

### 4.3 Usability (win 3)

Sessions with people, never agents: after a ten-minute introduction, each person says, unprompted, what is protected, what is pending, who can recover, and what a receipt reveals. The figure: the fraction of correct answers per question per system, with the number of people and the wallet build. Comprehension claims rest on these sessions alone (ADR section 10 item 3).

### 4.4 Operating cost (win 4)

| figure | how collected |
|---|---|
| transaction size in bytes, two-in two-out | the bytes of record on each chain (Money measured; Monero's and Zcash's measured on the same test and beside their published figures) |
| fee in the chain's gas or fee unit | the fee paid per transfer at the chain's floor, in its own unit, never converted |
| scanning time and bandwidth for a day at 1,000 and 10,000 transfers | mode C on a phone of record and a PC of record |
| verification time and memory per proof and per block on the minimum node | L-01's and L-04's figures; the comparable systems' on the same machine |
| proving time and memory on the PC and the phone | ADR 4.6's thresholds |
| storage per supported device after a year at the stated rate | measured on the prototype; published figures for the others |

## 5. What is not claimed

- A passing campaign proves the absence of the bugs it looked for and nothing else. Tests do not establish an unproven construction's security; the cryptographic review, the published bounds and the measurements of ADR section 4.6 are what the claim rests on.
- A row that passes on a throwaway chain passes on that chain; the research chain's run is the one of record.
- The comparable systems' figures are collected with their public releases on their test networks and may differ on their main networks; the record names the version and the network.
- A small network cannot manufacture an anonymity set (ADR section 10); every privacy figure carries the pool size and the rate it was measured at.
- The usability sessions' figures are the sessions'; no claim about comprehension is made from an engineer's own reading.
