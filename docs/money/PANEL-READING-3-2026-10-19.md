## 2. lead/money-128bit 6bb6ff03f (threshold 1 at 128 conjectured bits)

**Ruling: PASS WITH RE-CUTS (F-796, applied by the review on the page's status line).** The branch was cut from the join's pre-rewrite commit 0f260033f, which main carries as a0af623ca (the second reading's identity rewrite), so a merge saw two bases and re-raised the whole join; the panel took the branch's one commit by cherry-pick onto the integration branch (78d80aec6, patch-identical, 15 files), and says so here. The read, hardest on the patches and the formula:

- **The field.** The cubic extension F_p[X]/(X^3 - X - 1) of Goldilocks as the Miden VM proof's challenge field, delegated to p3-goldilocks 0.8.0's instance (its Frobenius matrix, its generator 2 + X, two-adicity 32) exactly as the crate delegates the quadratic and quintic instances (miden-field.patch, 45 lines); the four names re-exported through miden-crypto; miden-air's CubeFelt and the five `*_cubic_config` factories beside the release's quadratic ones; miden-prover's three type sites and five hash-function arms, miden-verifier's arms and bounds. The VM's own ext2 instructions keep QuadFelt, as they must. The lifted STARK, the lookup argument, DEEP, FRI, the challengers and the serialisation are generic over the extension and compile unchanged. Nothing in the statements, the witnesses, the hash, the encodings or the vectors changed: the prover's tests pass on the patched build (3 of 3 on lab box B tonight: the shared vectors, the statements' execute-and-refuse, the toolchain pin) and the program hashes are the join's.
- **The soundness argument.** The STARK's conjectured error terms are each of the form log2|E| minus a size term; a larger challenge field shrinks the lookup, composition, out-of-domain, DEEP and folding terms (from 108, 119, 113, 119, 115 bits at the quadratic field to 172.0, 183.3, 177.5, 182.7, 179.4 uncapped), which the cap at the commitment hash's 128 then binds; the query round is the one term the field does not move, and 38 queries at 2.967 bits each plus 17 bits of grinding read 129.7, so the minimum is the cap, 128. The panel recomputed every row of section 2's table from the stated inputs (log_max_height 11, the lookup shape (16 + 2) x 28 = 504, log2 428 constraints, 9 + 2 out-of-domain terms, LOG_BLOWUP 3, the grinding 17/12/4) and agrees to the figure. The arithmetic is consistent between the prover and the verifier by the 101 lab records verified and the forged-proof and moved-proof refusals; the field's own correctness rests on p3-goldilocks's tested instance, which the review reads as the standard assumption, not a new one. The proven bounds for this parameter class are far lower and the page says so; the bound named is the conjectured one, as the ADR requires.
- **The reporter's order (the page's section 6 item 1, the review's ruling).** The release computes the lookup term, caps it, then subtracts the boundary correction, so a round the cap binds reads 128 - 2^-13 and 127 whole bits whatever the field. The correction is part of the round's error (M = fractions_per_row x 2^H + boundary terms, the crate's own comment), so it belongs under the cap, as the budget applies the cap to every other round; below the cap the two orders agree to the bit (the join's 108 reproduces). the review holds the patch's order as the reading of record: the figure is 128 conjectured bits, and the release's order would read the same bound as 127 by a rounding, not a weaker round. F-796: the ruling written on the page's status line, since a reader of the page alone could not know which order the panel took.
- **The patch shas on the page**, f3655966010a362c (field), 6ad4fb6fee076907 (crypto), 8c8496d3b45dbd34 (air), 183411494c9c1e23 (prover), 27b0bf1c15ef8145 (verifier), are printed by prepare-patched.sh at every build; lab box B's run tonight printed the same five.
- **The one line outside the prover tree**: 7409: 1 in IgneumChainPoolFrom (the lab's gate); the live chains' entries absent, as before.
- **Listed, not faults:** (a) MinSecurityBits stays 108 in pool/verifier.go; the format refuses the join's proofs (measured: InvalidGrinding), so the constant is a belt, not the guard; the next node cut raises it to 128 with the verifier's version word. (b) In apply_lookup_correction the arm `Some(_) => 0` (a lookup with fractions_per_row 0) reads the round at zero bits where the release's order would not; unreachable for the Miden AIR, whose lookup shape is fixed at 28, named for the next patch.
