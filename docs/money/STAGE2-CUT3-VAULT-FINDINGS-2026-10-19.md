## 2. The three fixes, each with its failing-then-passing test

**F-799, the revocation at the announcement (74a8d63d3).** Rule 7 ("a completed recovery revokes every pending note of the old policy") never bound under the floors: rule 6 forbids a request after the announcement, so every pending note matured at h_req + D_b before the revocation bound at h_announce + R_b >= h_announce + 2 D_b. The cut: money-vault-announce-v1 reveals the old policy hash as a sixth public word (the circuit opens the policy to it; the host check and the wallet say "the policy does not hash to the revealed old policy hash" otherwise) and the node inserts its key into the revoked set in the announcing block; money-vault-rec-cancel-v1 reveals the same hash and the node removes it (revokedRemove: the leaf dropped, the next pointers relinked over the sorted keys, the last slot cleared, the root rewritten; the set is no longer append-only, and a removal returns the root to its earlier value); money-vault-policy-complete-v1 reveals it as before and the node checks it was revoked at the announcement and adds nothing. A completion is still proven against the revoked root as it stood at the parent block (F-778), so the revocation binds from the block after the announcement. The test: TestVaultRevocationAtTheAnnouncement (the announcement revokes and the parent root still binds in its own block; the next block reads the new root and refuses a completion against the older one; the policy completion names the announced hash and adds nothing, another hash is refused; a second vault's announcement then its cancellation removes its key, the leaves relinked, the root back to the one-key root; the last removal leaves the empty root): on main the announcement revokes nothing and the test fails at its first check; the prover's test refuses a wrong revealed word on announce and on rec-cancel (on main the word was not read and the refusal helper panics); the wallet's test reads the revealed hash on both records. Write sites: revokedRemove 4 added to tools/ci/finalize-writes.txt; the commit carries Fork-Gate: IgneumChainPoolFrom (every live chain at zero).

**F-800, the rotation only the authority can cancel (3c86dc7b5, re-cut ef8314ed7).** money-vault-rec-cancel-v1 admitted the policy's cancellation key against any pending recovery, so a griefer holding it could cancel the recovery that would rotate it (run 5 of the first lab prepared that cancellation). The cut: the circuit reads the announced policy (eight advice words; its hash must equal the head's rec_hash) and branches on its pk_c against the policy's: when it differs, the witness secret must open the policy's recovery authority (H(sk, TAG 17) = pk_a, not all-zero); when it is kept, the cancellation key as before (H(sk, TAG 16) = pk_c, not all-zero). THE RE-CUT (ef8314ed7, this session's finding, for the register): that form made run 4's attack uncancellable on the lab chain (the third start, quarantined as run3): a compromised recovery authority announces a policy with a new cancellation key, and the owner's cancellation key, the owner's one defence against the authority, was refused by the same rule that stops run 5's griefer. The reading's alternative tells the two apart: the everyday key's co-signature on the announcement. money-vault-announce-v1 now takes an optional co-signature (a bit in the advice; when set, sk_e and nk_e must open the policy's pk_e) and carries the bit in the head's IDX word (element 3, zero before and after a recovery, so it is in the commitment and cannot be forged on the witness); money-vault-rec-cancel-v1 reads it: the recovery authority's secret is required when the announced pk_c differs from the policy's AND the bit is 1 (the owner's rotation of a compromised cancellation key, run 5: the key it replaces cannot cancel it); the cancellation key's in every other case (a change that keeps it, or a rotation nobody co-signed: a compromised authority's, run 4, which the owner cancels). The everyday key still never announces or cancels; a thief holding it alone gains nothing from the bit (the announcement needs the authority's secret). The prover's host check and the wallet say the same words; the wallet's vault-announce co-signs when --wallet (the everyday wallet) is given, vault-rec-cancel takes either key by --key, needs the vault file's pending policy (the announced fields, which the announcer gives the cancellation key's holder: the chain carries only the hash), --force skips the wallet's own check for the lab's attacker row, and vault-scan shows co_signed; the head plaintext carries the bit at byte 173. The test: the prover's rotating recovery refused under sk_c and under a stranger's key and proven under sk_a; a kept-pk_c recovery (a second announcement) proven under sk_c and refused under sk_a; a witness naming another announced policy refused ("not the new policy"); on the F-799 commit the old key was admitted and the announced policy unread, so the first refusal assertion fails. The wallet's test: the same two branches on the vault file.

**F-801, the order inside a block (6c642ab70).** Every head-consuming statement names the head's nullifier, so an announcement proven against head_k was refused whenever the thief's one-wei request against head_k stood earlier in the block. The cut: Finalize applies a block's pool records in two passes (pool.Ordered, pool/order.go): money-vault-announce-v1, money-vault-rec-cancel-v1 and money-vault-cancel-v1 first, then every other record, each pass in transaction order; the appends follow the same order and Extract (the retained set, igneum_poolBlock, the pruned sync's check) walks it, so the tree positions agree on every node. A cancellation therefore also beats a completion of one pending note in one block. The test: TestVaultHeadRaceOrdered builds the racing block in both transaction orders (the announcement takes the head, the request is refused, the retained set lists the announcement at the position Finalize appended it) and the cancel-against-complete block, and reads one state root for both orders; on main the first transaction won. ## 7. The caveats

- The revocation binds from the block after the announcement (the completion's revoked root is the parent block's, F-778): a pending note that has matured and is proven and included in the announcing block itself completes. Under the floors a note matures D after its request, so the authority that announces inside D of the theft is never a block late; the case is a matured note the thief had not yet completed, which the announcement reaches one block later.
- After a recovery completes, the old policy's pending notes stay revoked and have no exit until money-vault-reclaim is built (the spec's S_reclaim; the first page's caveat stands). After a cancelled recovery they complete as before.
- A compromised recovery authority now freezes the owner's own pending withdrawals as well as new requests until the cancellation key clears it (run 4's row); the bound is unchanged (nothing moves), the griefing is up to R as before.
- The co-signature decides by who holds the everyday key: with the everyday key AND the cancellation key both compromised, the authority's rotation cannot be co-signed by the owner, so the stolen cancellation key can cancel it; the owner's remedy is then a fresh vault funded by the authority's non-rotating change of the everyday key... which the same stolen cancellation key can cancel too. That pair of compromises (both of the owner's keys) is outside the spec's section 9 table (one key stolen at a time) and is named here as the limit of the co-signature form, for the review.
- F-802 (the panel's fourth reading, docs/money/PANEL-READING-4-2026-10-19.md): the co-signed announcement is ONE witness carrying sk_a and sk_e together (the prover's advice reads SK_A, then the bit, then SK_E and NK_E; the wallet's prepare_announce puts both secrets in one witness file). A recovery authority that is a separate party (the spec's user-chosen authority, the t-of-n guardians of its section 4.2) cannot co-sign without holding the owner's everyday secret, and the owner cannot co-sign without the authority's; the lab's run 5 holds because one prover held both. The form is sound where the owner is its own authority and is the record's; the next cut splits the consent into the owner's own statement (the everyday key consumes the head and sets the bit with the announced policy's hash, before the authority's announcement) or carries two proofs in one record. The co-signer reads the new policy it is given: the wallet should print the changed fields (pk_c above all) before it co-signs.
- F-804 (the same reading): the announcement now reveals the old policy hash, which the spec kept private until S_rec_complete. The three records of one recovery (announce, rec-cancel or policy-complete) are therefore linkable on the ledger by the revealed word, and a holder of the vault's deposit address (vid, policy_hash, ek_vault: any depositor) learns that a recovery of that vault is under way from the announcing block, where before it learned so at the completion. The vault's other operations stay unlinkable as the first page says; the spec's re-cut carries this as the price of F-799.
- The cancellation of a rotating recovery needs the announced policy's fields in the vault file (the announcer shares them with the holder of the key that may cancel; the chain carries only the hash). A cancellation key's holder who has only the hash cannot build the witness, which is the rule's intent when pk_c rotates and a gap to document when it does not.
- The two-pass order is inside Finalize and Extract only; the transaction pool's order and the block's transaction order are unchanged. Shields and spends are second-pass records, so their positions move behind a block's first-pass records; nothing reads positions from the transaction order any more.
- Run 3 at the scaled delays is not a row of record (the driver's 60-block offset against the scaled 8-block margin); its full-scale rows are the re-run's.
- The partition (reorganisation) row was not run on this chain (two nodes; the re-run's and the first page's rows stand); the driver keeps it behind -reorg.
- Not measured: the circuits' row counts; the node's cost inside Finalize is read from S's log as before.
