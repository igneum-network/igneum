Mines Igneum with your graphics card. Set up in five short steps.

Pools

Fee, payout conditions, reliability (the pool's statement and the independent probe, both labelled) and concentration (its share of the network's work; a warning above a third). Ordered by concentration, then fee. Nothing is preselected. Pools operated by the project are labelled.

Solo

Solo keeps the whole block but pays rarely: the Earnings page says how rarely at your rate. It needs a node you run, or a remote node you trust (its operator sees your work and could withhold it).
